StatusCore documents public HTTP/HTTPS monitoring and status pages using Next.js, NestJS/Prisma, PostgreSQL and Redis/BullMQ. API schedulers queue checks and persisted notification deliveries; a separate non-HTTP worker executes both. Failure thresholds open automatic incidents, success resolves them; check/state/incident/outbox changes share a database transaction. Delivery runs asynchronously and is retryable, not exactly-once. Manual timelines do not alter monitor measurements. Networking validates public destinations/DNS and pins connections; checks revalidate redirects, while HTTPS notifications reject redirects. This is not an SSRF-immunity claim. One-time maintenance suppresses new outages while recording checks; metrics are observational, not maintenance-adjusted SLA guarantees. Recurring maintenance, multi-region probes, public historical analytics and retention/rollups are future work. Deployment guide targets do not establish a live deployment or independent security audit.
